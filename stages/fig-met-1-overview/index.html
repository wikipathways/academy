Published Pathway Figure: Metabolic Pathway - Overview

Background

This quest desribes the process of authoring a pathway model from existing information. The content underlying a pathway model can come from many different sources, for example published literature, your own research or other types of pathway information resources, like websites. Most commonly, the pathway information comes from one of more published articles.

To find a published paper with pathway information, here are some resources:

Pathway Figure OCR
Pathway Figure OCR is an open science project dedicated to extracting pathway information from the published literature to be freely used by anyone. This website lets you filter, search and view published pathways from literature and the information that was extracted. Search for your favorite gene, disease, or search by figure or pathway title.
WikiPathways Pathway Curation Tasks
A list of pathway topics requested by the WikiPathways community.
