Published Pathway Figure: Metabolic Pathway - Overview

Background

This path desribes the process of authoring a pathway model from existing information. The content underlying a pathway model can come from many different sources, for example published literature, your own research or other types of pathway information resources, like websites. Most commonly, the pathway information comes from one of more published articles.

Ideally, complete this path with a published figure of your own choosing. If not, follow along using the example pathway figure. If you are starting with a figure from the Pathway Figure OCR project, follow the Pathway Figure OCR path instead.
